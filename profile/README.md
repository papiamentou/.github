# Papiamentou

**Bon bini!** Welcome to Papiamentou, an open-source effort to digitize and build language tools for **Papiamento** and **Papiamentu**.

## Why "Papiamentou"?

Papiamento/Papiamentu is a creole language spoken in the ABC islands of the Caribbean. It has two standard spellings:

| | Papiamento | Papiamentu |
|---|---|---|
| **Where** | Aruba | Curaçao and Bonaire |
| **Spelling** | Mostly etymological (closer to Spanish/Portuguese roots) | Mostly phonological (written as it sounds) |
| **Example** | *Papiamento* | *Papiamentu* |

The name combines both. We treat the two as equal and aim to support **both spellings** in everything we build, without picking a "correct" one.

## What we do

- **Digitization**: turning printed and handwritten texts, recordings, and other materials into open, searchable, machine-readable formats.
- **Datasets**: building clean, well-documented text and speech corpora with clear licensing and sources.
- **Language tools**: spell checkers, dictionaries, keyboards, converters between the two spellings, and other everyday tools.
- **Language technology**: helping Papiamento/Papiamentu get proper support in translation, speech recognition, and AI models.

## Contributing

Anyone can contribute, whether you code or not. Native speakers, teachers, writers, linguists, archivists, and developers are all welcome. Ways to help:

- Share or point us to texts, recordings, or archives (with permission to use them)
- Review and correct data in either spelling
- Report problems or suggest new tools by opening an issue
- Send pull requests to any of our repositories

Please say which variant (Papiamento or Papiamentu) your contribution covers so we can label it correctly.
